Heron: Water Reminder, Tracker — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. Heron is made by NaRiSystems. Contact: narisystems@gmail.com.

The short version

There is no Heron account. Your drink log, weight and settings stay on your phone unless you choose to back them up to your own Google Drive. Heron shows no ads. Apart from that optional backup, the only information that leaves your phone is anonymous crash and usage information, described below, and Google Play handles any purchase.

What stays on your phone

Your drinks (amounts and times), weight, optional sex, wake and sleep times, reminder and display settings, and your goal are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Reset data, deletes them from the phone. Backups you made to your Google Drive stay there until you delete them.

Optional backup to your Google Drive

If you tap Back up to Google Drive in Settings, Heron asks you to sign in with Google and then saves a backup file (your drinks and settings) in a hidden folder in your own Google Drive. Only Heron can see that folder, it does not appear in your Drive file list, and the only permission requested is access to that app folder. We never receive the backup, and we do not see your Google account or email address. Heron only reads the email shown on screen so you can tell which account a backup belongs to. You can restore it on a new phone by signing in with the same account, and you can delete the backups from Google Drive settings (Manage apps) at any time. Backup happens only when you tap it.

Anonymous diagnostics

  • Crash reports (Sentry). If the app crashes, a report is sent containing the error details, your phone model, Android version and the app version. It does not contain your drink log, weight or settings, and is not linked to your name or any account.
  • Anonymous usage events (Aptabase). The app sends simple events such as "app opened" or "drink logged", with the app version, Android version and an approximate country. It does not send what you drank, when, or any personal details.

We use this only to find bugs and understand whether the app is working. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

Purchases

The optional Supporter purchase is processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app only learns whether a Supporter purchase exists on your Google account.

Notifications

Reminders are created on your phone. Nothing about them is sent to us.

Children

Heron is intended for adults aged 18 and over.

Not medical advice

Heron is a reminder and a log. It does not give medical advice. If a doctor has given you a fluid limit, follow that instead.

Your choices

You can delete all data at any time with Settings → Reset data, or by uninstalling. Because we hold no account or identifying information, there is nothing further we can look up or delete on our side. Backups in your Google Drive are yours to delete.
